# Common-Cyber-Threats
1. Definitions

Threat

A potential danger that can harm a computer, network, or data.

Vulnerability

A weakness in a system that attackers can exploit.

Attack

An attempt to exploit a vulnerability to steal data or damage a system.

2. Common Cyber Threats

Phishing

Explanation: Fake emails or messages used to steal personal information.

Example: A fake bank email asking for your login details.

Protection: Don’t click suspicious links, verify the sender, and use MFA.

Malware

Explanation: Malicious software that harms a computer.

Example: Downloading an infected file from an unknown website.

Protection: Use antivirus software and download only from trusted sources.

Ransomware

Explanation: Malware that locks your files and demands payment.

Example: A company loses access to its files until a ransom is paid.

Protection: Back up your files and keep your software updated.

Social Engineering

Explanation: Tricking people into giving away sensitive information.

Example: Someone pretending to be IT support asks for your password.

Protection: Verify identities and never share your password.

Password Attacks

Explanation: Attempts to guess or steal passwords.

Example: An attacker tries many common passwords to access an account.

Protection: Use strong passwords and enable MFA.

DoS/DDoS

Explanation: Flooding a website with traffic to make it unavailable.

Example: An online store crashes because of fake traffic.

Protection: Use firewalls, DDoS protection, and monitor network traffic.
